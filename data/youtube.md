- **Show:** МастерШеф (MasterChef Ukraine, channel STB) — **season 16**, aired 23.08.2025 – 27.12.2025
- **Result:** **Top 8**. Eliminated in **episode 15 (06.12.2025)** in the Black Apron battle — his breakfast dish failed the main condition, syrnyky were undercooked, flavours unbalanced. Halyna's salmon won the battle.
- **Age / city:** 35, from Тарту / Таллінн, Estonia (Kalamaja district); previously ~8 years in Kyiv.

- **Official profile:** https://www.stb.ua/masterchef/ua/member/garik-nosach/
- **After the show:** reported plans for a Ukrainian-Asian food truck; judge Olia Martynovska picked him for an internship at her restaurant, which a fan channel later reported did not go ahead.
