## Who he is

An Estonian-born chef working in Ukraine, and a contestant on season 16 of the Ukrainian
**MasterChef**. The single most load-bearing fact of the brand is the career switch: **ten years
in finance, then a hard pivot into professional cooking.** He puts it in the bio, in the name
line, and it is the emotional spine of his highest-performing post (17k likes) about winning a
stage/internship — "Я виграв не просто стажування, я виграв, без перебільшення— нове життя"
("I didn't just win an internship, I won — no exaggeration — a new life").

He signs his chef whites with both flags, Estonian and Ukrainian, and the chest embroidery just
reads **ГАРІК** — first-name-only, which is how the whole brand is pitched.

## Services / offerings, as evidenced by the posts

With 60 posts in view (vs. the first 12), the commercial picture is broader than "a chef who does
pop-ups." There are **four distinct revenue lines**, and the recipe content is the engine feeding
all of them.

1. **DOUBLPUF (@doublpuf) — a recurring Kyiv street-food venture.** This is the single biggest
   discovery from the deeper fetch and was completely invisible in the first 12 posts. He co-runs
   a weekend street-food operation at **Гончара 38А, Kyiv**, grill on at 18:00, promoted as weekly
   "поставки" (drops) under the hashtag **#doublpufnow** (7 posts). Rotating formats: smash
   burgers "на рюмці", **Italian piadine** (п'ядіни) with chicken/truffle mayo, prosciutto crudo
   with pesto and peach, chorizo with double mozzarella; and Asian **satay** over open fire at
   party events. Operationally candid: "Видаємо тільки на місці. Без бронювання. Готівка в
   пріоритеті" (served on-site only, no reservations, cash preferred).
2. **Pop-up dinners and residencies** — in his display name. Multi-day residencies in other
   venues across cities: **Ivano-Frankivsk** (18–21 June, four-course author's menu), **Dnipro**
   (Dmytra Yavornytskoho 59a), plus season-opening pop-ups in Kyiv. The Ivano-Frankivsk menu is
   documented in full: beetroot carpaccio in cherry marinade with local bryndza; lyulya kebab with
   new potatoes and confit-garlic sour cream; chicken thigh with burnt honey, roasted-corn cream
   and "Zhu" sauce; dessert "Франківський дворик".
3. **Brand cheffing / sponsored recipe integrations — now concretely evidenced.** This is a real,
   active revenue line, not just a bio claim. Paid or partnered integrations appear with:
   **Braun** (@braunhouseholdua — Multiquick 9 hand blender, Multifry 5), **Stozhar** cooking oil,

   beef), **Nani Sushi**, **Uman Pyvo** (ambassador), and even **adidas Ukraine** (non-food
   lifestyle). Format is consistent: a genuine full recipe built around the partner's product.
4. **Creative Chef, Yuzu City Cafe (@yuzu.citycafe)** — an ongoing restaurant role with the venue
   mid-launch: tastings, first tableware, "first form", deliberate teasers, August opening.
   Founder credited as @anastasiia_chernenko_.

Still **not** evidenced anywhere in 60 posts: cooking classes, private in-home chef bookings,

## Vibe and tone

- **Monochrome and stark.** The pop-up key art is black-and-white, with a hand-drawn oval
  logotype reading **ГАРІК НОСАЧ** plus loose sketched "spark" marks. Kitchen photography is
  moody and low-lit. He is consistently in a black chef jacket or black tee; the tattooed
  forearms are part of the look. This suggests a site palette of near-black, off-white, one warm
  accent — not a bright, cheerful food-blog aesthetic.
- **Warm, emotional, first-person.** Captions are gratitude-forward and unguarded. He thanks
  mentors at length, credits collaborators by handle, and writes about self-belief: "іноді в тебе
  вірять більше, ніж ти сам в себе" ("sometimes people believe in you more than you believe in
  yourself"). The contrast between the severe visual identity and the soft, sincere copy is the
  brand's actual signature — the landing page should preserve both, not smooth them into one.
- **Momentum and anticipation.** Recurring register: "Ще один крок ближче" (one step closer),
  "Продовжую будувати шлях" (I keep building the path), "Якби ми показали більше — було б уже не
  цікаво" (if we showed more it wouldn't be interesting anymore), "See you soon". He sells the
  becoming as much as the food.
- **Community-first, low-hierarchy.** Pop-up recaps talk about guests' warm meetings and
  conversations rather than about technique. "Ми з Гаріком раді кожному" — we're glad to see
  everyone.

    `https://www.instagram.com/accounts/login/?next=/api/v1/highlights/232816708/highlights_tray/`

    `{"message":"invalid request", ... "summary":"Incorrect Query"}` (the legacy hash is retired)
  - `GET /api/v1/feed/reels_tray/` -> **HTTP 302** to login
  Highlight titles and covers (pop-ups, Portugal, daily outfits) are visible to a logged-in
  browser but cannot be retrieved without authentication. `instagram.json` carries an empty
  `highlights: []` array plus a `highlights_note` recording these exact failures.
- **Video files.** 43 of 60 posts are Reels, so `post-NN.jpg` is a video cover frame rather than a
  photograph. The underlying MP4s were not downloaded.
- **Posts 61-256.** Pagination stopped at 60 by design (`more_available` was still `true`, so
  deeper paging does appear to work unauthenticated if more is ever wanted).
- **Avatar resolution caps at 320x320** — too small for a large hero portrait. Better to crop from
  post-05 or post-10, both clean, well-lit shots of him, or from the Portugal set.
- No email, phone, website, or booking link is published anywhere on the profile. **Contact
  details will have to come from the client directly.**
